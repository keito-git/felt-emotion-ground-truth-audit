# S6. Audit protocol as frozen before data collection

This file summarises the protocol that was fixed before the collection of data. Changes made after
freezing were recorded as additions together with their reasons and dates, and the two additions
that were made are given at the end.

## Sampling frame

The frame is the union of the inclusion tables of surveys that satisfy four rules, namely that the
survey is peer reviewed, that it explicitly provides an inclusion table of datasets, that its
inclusion criteria are stated in the text, and that it is fixed before coding begins. The
classifications given by the surveys are used only to define the frame. Every item is coded
independently from primary documents, and divergence between a survey's classification and the
coding of this study is tallied separately.

## Inclusion and exclusion

Included are resources that carry human labels of the affective state of a person, that are
publicly available or available on application for academic use, and whose dataset paper or
datasheet is public. Excluded are resources whose object is not a person, resources whose labels
are entirely machine generated, and resources whose primary documents cannot be obtained. Counts
and reasons for the last category are recorded.

## Coding

Judgements are made only from primary documents, namely the dataset paper, the datasheet and the
distribution page. Every judgement carries a verbatim quotation and its location. Where no
statement can be confirmed, the item is recorded as undecidable together with the places searched,
so that the absence of a statement is distinguished from a failure to find one.

## Downstream audit

Citing papers are retrieved in descending order of citation count. Papers that describe themselves
as surveys, papers whose purpose is to present a new resource, and papers that do not actually use
the dataset are excluded by rule. Papers whose full text cannot be obtained are recorded as
exclusions and the next paper in the ranking is examined. The sampling rule fixes the target at 20
analysed papers per dataset; where the candidates are exhausted before that target, the count
reached is reported.

## Additions recorded after freezing

**Addition 1.** The composition of the frame was closed, the three surveys were named, and the
absence of a survey specialised in speech was recorded as a gap that is reported as a limitation.

**Addition 2.** The coding values for the downstream audit were specified in more detail after a
pilot showed that downstream papers do not state which label they use. The sampling rule itself was
not changed. The retrieval interface was changed because of rate limits, again without changing the
rule.
